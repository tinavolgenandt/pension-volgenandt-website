## 3. Google Ads -- Feasibility & ROI Analysis

### 3.1 Cost Estimates

| Metric | Value | Source |
|---|---|---|
| Germany average CPC | 0.62 EUR | searchads.agency Q3 2025 |
| Travel sector average CPC | 0.82 EUR | searchads.agency Q3 2025 |
| Rural Thuringia estimated CPC | **0.30-0.60 EUR** | 30-50% below national avg due to low competition |
| Accommodation conversion rate | 3.5-4.2% | roomstay.io benchmarks |
| Average booking value (Pension Volgenandt) | ~135 EUR (2 nights) | Based on room mix |

### 3.2 Break-Even Calculation

```
Average CPC:                    0.50 EUR
Conversion rate:                3.5%
Clicks needed per booking:      1 / 0.035 = 29 clicks
Cost per booking:               29 x 0.50 = 14.50 EUR
Cost as % of booking value:     14.50 / 135 = 10.7%
```

### 3.3 Channel Cost Comparison (per 135 EUR booking)

| Channel | Cost per Booking | % of Revenue | Risk |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Organic SEO** | ~0 EUR | 0% | No cost but takes 3-6 months |
| **Google Ads (Search)** | ~14.50 EUR | 10.7% | CPC risk, requires management |
| **Google Free Booking Links** | 0 EUR | 0% | Free but requires Beds24-Google Hotel Center connection |
| **Booking.com** | 16-20 EUR | 12-15% | Zero upfront risk, pay per booking |
| **Booking.com Preferred** | 20-24 EUR | 15-18% | Higher visibility but expensive |
| **Airbnb** | ~4 EUR (host fee) | 3% | Guest pays ~14% extra, limiting bookings |
| **Monteurzimmer portals** | ~10 EUR/month flat | <1% at volume | Low cost, niche audience |

**Verdict: Google Ads is cheaper per booking than Booking.com, but organic SEO + Google Free Booking Links should be the first priority since they're free.**
